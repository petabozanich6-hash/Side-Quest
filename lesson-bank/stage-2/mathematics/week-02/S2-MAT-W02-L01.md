## Teach
### Adding in parts
Welcome back, {{name}}. To add two three-digit numbers, split each into hundreds, tens and ones, then add the matching parts. This is called adding by partitioning.
### Worked method
To add 234 and 125, add the hundreds: 200 + 100 = 300. Add the tens: 30 + 20 = 50. Add the ones: 4 + 5 = 9. Then put the answers together: 300 + 50 + 9 = 359.
### Why it works
The order of adding does not change the total. Adding hundreds with hundreds, tens with tens and ones with ones keeps every part in its own place, which is why place value matters.
### When the parts are easy
This method is easiest when no place adds to more than 9. In the next lessons you will learn what to do when a place adds to 10 or more.
## Worked examples
- 234 + 125 :: 200 + 100 = 300, 30 + 20 = 50, 4 + 5 = 9, so 300 + 50 + 9 :: 359
- 412 + 356 :: 400 + 300 = 700, 10 + 50 = 60, 2 + 6 = 8 :: 768
- 530 + 245 :: 500 + 200 = 700, 30 + 40 = 70, 0 + 5 = 5 :: 775
