## Teach
### Adding near numbers
Numbers close to 10 or 100 are easier to add if you round first and then fix the answer. To add 9, add 10 and take 1 away. To add 99, add 100 and take 1 away. To add 98, add 100 and take 2 away.
### The jump strategy
On a number line, start with the biggest number and jump by the parts of the other number. To add 346 and 125: 346 + 100 = 446, then + 20 = 466, then + 5 = 471.
### Doubles and near doubles
If you know a double you can use it. 150 + 150 = 300. For near doubles, use the double and adjust: 250 + 260 is double 250 (500) plus 10 more, which is 510.
### Choosing a strategy
No single method is best. Look at the numbers first, {{name}}. If one is close to a hundred use compensation, and if they are close to each other use doubles. Otherwise use jumps or written regrouping.
