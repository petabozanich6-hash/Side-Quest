## Teach
### Partitioning
Final lesson of the week, {{name}}. You will split numbers in new ways, then show what you have learned. Partitioning means splitting a number into parts. We have already split numbers into hundreds, tens and ones. 346 = 300 + 40 + 6.
### Flexible partitioning
A number can be split in other ways too. 346 = 200 + 140 + 6 because 1 hundred has been regrouped as 10 tens. It also equals 300 + 30 + 16 because 1 ten has been regrouped as 10 ones. The total stays the same.
### Why it matters
Flexible partitioning is what we use when we add and subtract. In the next weeks you will use it to regroup when you subtract from numbers such as 302.
### Checking your answer
To check a partition, add the parts. If they make the number, the partition is right.
## Worked examples
- Show 352 as 2 hundreds and some tens and ones. :: Take one hundred from 3 hundreds. That leaves 2 hundreds and makes 10 more tens. 5 tens + 10 tens = 15 tens. :: 2 hundreds, 15 tens, 2 ones
- Show 421 as 4 hundreds, 1 ten and ___ ones. :: Take one ten from 2 tens. 1 ten is left. 10 ones plus 1 one is 11 ones. :: 4 hundreds, 1 ten, 11 ones
- Is 500 + 60 + 5 the same as 400 + 160 + 5? :: 400 + 160 = 560. 500 + 60 = 560. :: Yes, both equal 565
