## Teach
### Compare place by place
Today, {{name}}, you will learn how to compare and order numbers like a mathematician. To compare two numbers, start with the biggest place, the hundreds. The number with more hundreds is bigger. If the hundreds are the same, compare the tens. If the tens are the same too, compare the ones.
### Symbols
The symbol > means 'is greater than' and < means 'is less than'. The open mouth faces the bigger number. 394 > 349 and 349 < 394. The symbol = means 'is equal to'.
### Ordering
Ascending order goes from smallest to largest. Descending order goes from largest to smallest. To order several numbers, compare their hundreds first, then tens, then ones.
### Careful with the digits
A number with more digits is not always about the digits themselves. 612 has digits 6, 1 and 2, but 621 is bigger because its tens digit is bigger. Always compare the same places.
## Worked examples
- Compare 349 and 394. :: Hundreds are both 3. Tens: 4 is less than 9. :: 349 < 394
- Compare 508 and 485. :: Hundreds: 5 is more than 4. :: 508 > 485
- Put 327, 273, 372 in ascending order. :: Hundreds: 2 then 3 and 3. Between 327 and 372, tens: 2 is less than 7. :: 273, 327, 372
## Guided practice
