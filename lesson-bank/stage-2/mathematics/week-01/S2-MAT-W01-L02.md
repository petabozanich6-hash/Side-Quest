- What is the value of the 4 in 640? :: 40
## Teach
### Expanded form
Welcome back, {{name}}. Today you will write numbers in expanded form and see why zero matters. Expanded form shows a number as the sum of its parts. 346 = 300 + 40 + 6. Each part is the value of one digit.
### Zero holds a place
In 407 there are 4 hundreds, 0 tens and 7 ones. The zero holds the tens place so the 4 stays in the hundreds place. Without it, 47 and 407 would look the same.
### Expanded form with zeros
We can leave out parts that are zero. 407 = 400 + 0 + 7, or just 400 + 7. 350 = 300 + 50.
### Going backwards
To turn expanded form back into a number, write each part in its place. 600 + 20 + 5 is 625. 800 + 3 is 803, because there are no tens.
## Worked examples
- Write 725 in expanded form. :: 7 is in the hundreds place: 700. 2 is in the tens place: 20. 5 is in the ones place: 5. :: 700 + 20 + 5
- Write 900 + 6 as a number. :: 9 hundreds, no tens, 6 ones. :: 906
- Which number is 500 + 40? :: 5 hundreds, 4 tens, no ones. :: 540
