## Teach
### 10 more, 10 less
Good to see you, {{name}}. Today you will find patterns when you count on and back by 10 and 100. Adding 10 changes only the tens digit by one. 10 more than 342 is 352. 10 less than 342 is 332. When the tens digit is 9, 10 more makes a new hundred: 10 more than 392 is 402.
### 100 more, 100 less
Adding 100 changes only the hundreds digit by one. 100 more than 342 is 442. 100 less than 342 is 242.
### Skip counting
Skip counting means counting in jumps. Counting by 10s from 240 gives 240, 250, 260, 270. Counting by 100s from 150 gives 150, 250, 350, 450. Counting backwards uses less each time.
### Patterns on the number line
On a number line, equal jumps are the same length. Jumps of 10 land on numbers that have the same ones digit. Jumps of 100 land on numbers with the same tens and ones digits.
## Worked examples
- What is 10 more than 475? :: Tens digit 7 becomes 8. :: 485
- What is 100 less than 630? :: Hundreds digit 6 becomes 5. :: 530
- Continue the pattern: 320, 340, 360, ___, ___ :: The jumps are 20 each time. :: 380, 400
## Guided practice

- E. A pattern starts at 210 and counts by 20s. Write the first six numbers. Which numbers have a 0 in the ones place? Why? :: E. 210, 230, 250, 270, 290, 310. All of them have 0 in the ones place, because adding 20 never changes the ones digit.
## Quiz
- 10 more than 395 is :: 405 | 495 | 396 :: 405 :: Tens digit 9 plus one makes ten tens, so a new hundred.
- 100 less than 507 is :: 407 | 497 | 517 :: 407 :: Hundreds digit goes down by one.
- What comes next? 610, 710, 810, ___ :: 820 | 910 | 900 :: 910 :: The pattern is plus 100.
- 10 less than 1000 is :: 990 | 900 | 999 :: 990 :: 1000 is 100 tens, and taking away one ten leaves 99 tens.
