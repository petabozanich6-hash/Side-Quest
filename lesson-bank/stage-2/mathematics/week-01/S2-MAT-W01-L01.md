## Teach
### Numbers are made of parts
Welcome, {{name}}. Today you will learn how every three-digit number is built from hundreds, tens and ones. Every three-digit number is made of hundreds, tens and ones. The number 346 has 3 hundreds, 4 tens and 6 ones.
### Place value
The place a digit sits in tells you how much it is worth. In 346 the 3 is in the hundreds place so it is worth 300. The 4 is in the tens place so it is worth 40. The 6 is in the ones place so it is worth 6.
### Show it with blocks
A flat is 100, a long is 10 and a small cube is 1. The number 346 is 3 flats, 4 longs and 6 cubes. Ten cubes make one long, and ten longs make one flat.
### Reading and writing numbers
We read 346 as three hundred and forty-six. The word 'and' comes after the hundreds. We write the digits in order: hundreds, then tens, then ones.
## Worked examples
- Write 5 hundreds, 2 tens and 8 ones as a number. :: Put 5 in the hundreds place, 2 in the tens place and 8 in the ones place. :: 528
- What is the value of the 7 in 372? :: Find the place of the 7. It is the middle digit, so it is in the tens place. :: 70
- How many hundreds, tens and ones are in 614? :: Read each digit by its place: 6, 1, 4. :: 6 hundreds, 1 ten, 4 ones
