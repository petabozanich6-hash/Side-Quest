## Part 1: Recall (5 min)
1. What is the opposite operation of addition? Answer: subtraction.
2. Which is larger, 3.5 or 3.25? Answer: 3.5.
3. Find 17 + 8 - 5. Answer: 20.
4. What does the word 'whole number' mean? Answer: a non-negative number with no fraction part (0, 1, 2, 3...).

## Part 2: Teach (15 min)

Card 1, What is an integer? Integers are the whole numbers and their negatives: ... -3, -2, -1, 0, 1, 2, 3 ... They have no fraction or decimal part. Zero is an integer and is neither positive nor negative.

Card 2, Why we need negatives. Negative numbers describe values below zero: temperatures below freezing, a bank account that is overdrawn, a depth below sea level, or a tide height below the chart datum.

Card 3, The number line. Zero sits in the middle. Positive integers go to the right, negative integers to the left. The further right a number sits, the greater it is. So -2 is greater than -7.

Card 4, Opposites. Two numbers the same distance from zero on opposite sides are opposites. The opposite of 5 is -5. The opposite of -9 is 9. Zero is its own opposite.

Card 5, Absolute value. The absolute value of a number is its distance from zero, written |n|. |-6| = 6 and |6| = 6. Distance is never negative.

Card 6, Comparing and ordering. Use the symbols < (less than) and > (greater than). Example: -4 < -1 because -4 is further left. Order from smallest to largest: -8, 3, -2, 0 becomes -8, -2, 0, 3.

Card 7, Worked examples.
Example 1: Which is warmer, -3 degrees or -9 degrees? -3 is further right, so -3 degrees is warmer.
Example 2: A diver is at -14 m and rises 6 m. Where is she now? Moving right by 6 gives -8 m.
Example 3: List in order, smallest first: 5, -1, -10, 0, -3. Answer: -10, -3, -1, 0, 5.

## Part 5: Independent task (20 min)
The tide table for the Lost Island shows heights in metres relative to the average sea level.

| Time | 6 am | 9 am | 12 pm | 3 pm | 6 pm | 9 pm |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Height (m) | 2 | 0 | -2 | -3 | -1 | 1 |

A. Draw a number line from -4 to 3 and mark each height. (Photo upload.)
B. Order the six heights from lowest to highest.
C. At which time is the water lowest? How far below average sea level is it?
D. The cave floor is at -2 m. For how many of the six times is the cave floor underwater? (A time is underwater when the tide is higher than -2 m.)
E. Write a number sentence for each change: 6 am to 12 pm, and 3 pm to 9 pm. Say the change in height.
F. The explorer needs the tide at least 1 m above average to leave the island by boat. Which times work?

Answers: B -3, -2, -1, 0, 1, 2. C 3 pm, 3 m below. D 4 times: 6 am, 9 am, 6 pm, 9 pm, because they are above -2... check: -2 itself equals the floor (not underwater), 12 pm is not underwater; heights above -2 are 2, 0, -1, 1, so 4 times. E 2 to -2 is a fall of 4 m; -3 to 1 is a rise of 4 m. F 6 am and 9 pm... 9 pm is 1 m, which meets 'at least 1', so 6 am, 9 pm.

Success checklist: marks are in the right place on the number line; each answer uses units; each answer is explained in a sentence.
