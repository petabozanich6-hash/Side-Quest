## Part 1: Warm-up (5 min)
1. Count by tens from 30 to 90. Answer: 30, 40, 50, 60, 70, 80, 90.
2. What is 10 more than 47? Answer: 57.
3. What is 10 less than 62? Answer: 52.
4. How many tens in 50? Answer: 5.

## Part 2: Teach (15 min)

Card 1, Numbers are made of parts. Every three-digit number is made of hundreds, tens and ones. The number 346 has 3 hundreds, 4 tens and 6 ones.

Card 2, Place value. The place a digit sits in tells you how much it is worth. In 346, the 3 is in the hundreds place so it is worth 300. The 4 is in the tens place so it is worth 40. The 6 is in the ones place so it is worth 6.

Card 3, Show it with blocks. A flat is 100, a long is 10 and a small cube is 1. 346 is 3 flats, 4 longs and 6 cubes.

Card 4, Expanded form. We can write a number as the sum of its parts: 346 = 300 + 40 + 6.

Card 5, Zero is a place holder. In 407 there are 4 hundreds, 0 tens and 7 ones. The zero holds the tens place so the 4 stays in the hundreds. 407 and 47 are very different numbers.

Card 6, Worked examples.
Example 1: Write 5 hundreds, 2 tens and 8 ones. Answer: 528.
Example 2: What is the value of the 7 in 372? The 7 is in the tens place, so it is worth 70.
Example 3: Write 906 in expanded form. 900 + 0 + 6, or 900 + 6.

## Part 3: Guided practice (10 min)
1. How many hundreds, tens and ones are in 258? Hint: say each digit's place. Answer: 2, 5, 8.
2. Write the number: 6 hundreds, 0 tens, 3 ones. Hint: zero holds a place. Answer: 603.
3. What is the value of the 8 in 184? Hint: which place is it in? Answer: 80.
4. Write 725 in expanded form. Answer: 700 + 20 + 5.
5. Which is greater, 349 or 394? Hint: compare hundreds first, then tens. Answer: 394.

## Part 4: Break (3 min)
Stand up and stretch.

## Part 5: Independent task (15 min)
The cave has number doors. Complete the three tasks.

Task A, Build it. Write the number for each: (a) 4 hundreds 1 ten 9 ones, (b) 8 hundreds 0 tens 5 ones, (c) 2 hundreds 7 tens 0 ones, (d) 9 hundreds 9 tens 9 ones.
Answers: 419, 805, 270, 999.

Task B, Break it. Write each in expanded form: (a) 563, (b) 740, (c) 608.
Answers: 500 + 60 + 3, 700 + 40, 600 + 8.

Task C, Door code. The code has these clues: it is between 400 and 500; the tens digit is double the ones digit; the ones digit is 3. What is the code? Answer: 463. Explain how you found it in a sentence.

Success checklist: I checked every zero. I said each digit's place. I wrote my explanation in a full sentence.

## Part 6: Quiz (7 min)
1. In 482, the 8 is worth (a) 8 (b) 80 (c) 800. Answer b.
2. 300 + 50 + 4 equals (a) 354 (b) 345 (c) 3054. Answer a.
3. Which number has 6 tens? (a) 160 (b) 616 (c) 606... check: 160 has 6 tens, 616 has 1 ten, 606 has 0 tens. Answer a.
4. 5 hundreds and 9 ones is written as (a) 59 (b) 509 (c) 590. Answer b.
5. Which is the smallest? (a) 245 (b) 254 (c) 425. Answer a.
