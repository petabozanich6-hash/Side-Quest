# S2-W01-D1-L1-ENG

**Child:** Oliver | **Stage 2 | Term 4 | Week 1 | Day 1 | Lesson 1 | English**

**Quest title:** The Map Maker's Clue
**Plain title:** Reading for information
**Goal for Oliver:** Find the important facts in a short text and use them to answer questions.

**Parent only:** Outcome EN2-RECOM-01 (comprehends independently read texts). Rationale: first lesson builds the habit of rereading to find evidence. Evidence: quiz score, task response.

## Part 1: Warm-up (5 min)
Question 1: What is a sentence? (Hint: it has a naming part and a doing part.) Answer: a group of words that makes a complete idea.
Question 2: Which one is a sentence? 'The big boat' or 'The boat sailed away'. Answer: the second.
Question 3: What do we call the very first word of a sentence? Answer: it starts with a capital letter.

## Part 2: Teach (15 min)
Card 1, What is a clue? A clue is a small piece of information that helps you work something out. Explorers read maps and letters for clues. Readers do the same with texts.
Card 2, Key facts. Key facts are the most important pieces of information in a text, such as who, what, where and when. Not every word is a key fact.
Card 3, How to find them. Step 1: Read the whole text once. Step 2: Read it again slowly. Step 3: Underline words that answer who, what, where, when. Step 4: Ask, does this help me answer the question?
Card 4, Worked example. Text: 'Captain Ana sailed to a small island on Monday. She found a cave near the beach.' Who? Captain Ana. Where? A small island. When? Monday. What did she find? A cave near the beach.

## Part 3: Guided practice (10 min)
Text: 'Ben packed a rope, a torch and a water bottle. He walked to the cave before sunset.'
Q1 Who packed the bag? Hint: look for a name. Answer: Ben.
Q2 Name two things Ben packed. Hint: read the first sentence. Answer: any two of rope, torch, water bottle.
Q3 When did he walk to the cave? Hint: look for a time word. Answer: before sunset.

## Part 4: Break (3 min)
Stand up and stretch. Come back when the timer ends.

## Part 5: Independent task (15 min)
Read the explorer's note below, then answer in the boxes.

'To whoever finds this note. My name is Captain Ana. My ship crashed on a rocky island. I am safe, but I have no food. I have hidden my map under a big flat rock near the stream. Please come before the tide comes in on Friday.'

1. Who wrote the note?
2. Where did the ship crash?
3. What does she need?
4. Where is the map hidden?
5. When must a rescuer come? Copy the words from the text that tell you.

Success checklist: I read the note twice. I underlined key facts. My answers use words from the note.
Response type: text boxes.

Parent option: choose a reading for a longer text later.

## Part 6: Quiz (7 min, auto-marked)
1. A clue is (a) a small piece of information that helps you work something out (b) a long story (c) a picture. Answer a.
2. Key facts answer who, what, where and when. True or false? True.
3. In the note, where is the map? Options: under a flat rock, in the cave, on the ship. Answer: under a flat rock.
4. What should you do after reading a text once? Options: stop, read it again slowly, skip it. Answer: read it again slowly.
5. Which word tells you when? Options: stream, Friday, rock. Answer: Friday.
Feedback shows the explanation after each answer.
