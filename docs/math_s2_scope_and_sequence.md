# Stage 2 Mathematics: 50-week scope and sequence

Syllabus: NSW Mathematics K-10 (2022), Stage 2 (20 outcomes, Year 3 and Year 4 content combined).
Time: 50 weeks x 4 one-hour lessons = 200 hours. NESA advice: Maths at least 20% of total time.
Design: spiral. Each strand returns several times at rising difficulty, with Year 3 and Year 4 content mixed.

Weekly shape:
- Lessons 1-2: Number thread.
- Lesson 3: Measurement, Space and Data thread.
- Lesson 4: Mixed problem-solving and reasoning (MAO-WM-01) with retrieval of earlier weeks.

Resources: embedded video where possible; otherwise direct links to the exact page on Khan Academy or BBC Bitesize. No Department of Education material.
