## Decisions and non-negotiables
- One canonical existing Side-Quest family account, verified parent sign-in, multiple learners; no subscription required for tutoring or English Club. No second permanent account or login silo.
- Homeschool membership/preview, tutoring bookings and English Club enrolments are independent entitlements; expiring one must not disable the other. Retain existing subscriber, student, purchase, lesson, progress and billing records.
- A personalised private family portal with relevant services, appointments, proposals/agreements, payments, messages, resources and approved progress; private owner notes never exposed. Child sessions do not confer parent billing, messages, contact or agreement access.
- Owner HQ is one cohesive operational workspace with separate navigable task pages, clear next action, back navigation and saved state; not stacked forms/tabs or a new layer of duplicate admin tools.
- Owner reviews invoice details and explicitly approves issue/publication. Square handles payment; no card data in Side-Quest. Provider-confirmed, exact mapped payments may update booking automatically; partial/ambiguous/refunded/disputed payments require safe reconciliation. Keep manual Square issue/reconciliation fallback; never auto-publish or cancel an invoice without authorised rules and verification.
- AUD rates: tutoring $25/30min, $35/45min, $45/60min; written plans from $30, written support from $15; evening same rates, packages sum with no automatic discount. Club AU$12 per learner per 60-minute weekly session, up to eight, suggested ages 8–16, no compulsory term or membership. Flexible diary, no mandatory seven-day notice.
- Private staging uses synthetic accounts, separate D1/R2 and sandbox credentials. No production migration, backup attestation, live Square transaction, paid enrolment, publication or activation merely because CI passed.
