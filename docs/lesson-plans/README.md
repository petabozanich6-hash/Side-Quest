# Lesson plan base documents

One file per NESA stage, giving the time allocation and subject plan that every premade lesson set is built to. Lessons are not written until the plan for that stage and subject is checked.

## How stages work

NESA stages in primary and Years 7-10 span two years. NSW Department sample programs use an 8-term-per-stage structure, so Stages 1 to 5 are planned over two school years. Early Stage 1 (Kindergarten) is one year, 4 terms. Stage 6 is planned as Year 11 (Preliminary) and Year 12 (HSC). All stages assume 40 teaching weeks a year, 4 terms of 10 weeks.
