# Stage 4 priority plan (Years 7-8, 8 terms)

Per-year hours: English 160, Mathematics 160, Science 120, History 60, Geography 60, Technology 7-8 120, Language 100 (one 12-month period), two electives 120 each. See stage-4.md for the table and stage-4-5 subject files for focus areas.

## Confirmed Stage 4 outcome codes

### Science 7-10 (2023, in use from 2026)

Working scientifically: SC4-WS-01 Observing, SC4-WS-02 Questioning and predicting, SC4-WS-03 Planning investigations, SC4-WS-04 Conducting investigations, SC4-WS-05 Processing data and information, SC4-WS-06 Analysing data and information, SC4-WS-07 Problem-solving, SC4-WS-08 Communicating.

Content: SC4-OTU-01 (observations of the Universe), SC4-FOR-01 (forces in everyday contexts), SC4-CLS-01 (cells and classification), SC4-SOL-01 (separating substances), SC4-LIV-01 (living systems), SC4-PRT-01 (elements and compounds), SC4-CHG-01 (energy causing geological and chemical change), SC4-DA1-01 (data used to model and predict).

### History 7-10 (2024, from 2027)

HI4-CON-01 (continuity and change), HI4-SPE-01 (key features of past societies), HI4-CPP-01 (contexts and perspectives), HI4-IEP-01 (significant ideas and events), HI4-APP-01 (Aboriginal Peoples' experiences of colonisation), HI4-SOU-01 (evidence from sources), HI4-INQ-01 (meaning and context of sources), HI4-COM-01 (communicating historical ideas).

### Geography 7-10 (2024, from 2027)

GE4-DFC-01 (features of places and environments), GE4-PRI-01 (processes and interactions), GE4-PER-01 (perspectives), GE4-MAN-01 (management and protection), GE4-APC-01 (Aboriginal Peoples' Custodianship of Country), GE4-TAP-01 (geographical tools), GE4-COM-01 (communication).

Stage 5 codes for the same subjects follow the same pattern: SC5-, HI5-, GE5-. Stage 5 Science content codes are SC5-EGY-01, DIS-01, MAT-01, ENV-01, GEV-01 and 02, RXN-01 and 02, WAM-01 and 02, DA2-01. Stage 5 History includes HI5-APP-01 on Aboriginal Peoples' struggle for rights and freedoms.

Important timing: Science 7-10 (2023) is already in use. History and Geography 7-10 (2024) start in 2027, so for a student in Year 7 or 8 this year, the old syllabuses apply until then.
