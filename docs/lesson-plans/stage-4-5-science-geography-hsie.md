# Science 7-10, Geography 7-10 and HSIE K-6 plans

Focus areas copied from the NESA NSW Curriculum site. Hour splits are planning choices.

## Science 7-10 (2023 syllabus, implemented from 2026)

The 2018 syllabus is not available after December 2027, so plan to the 2023 version. It has 16 focus areas, 8 per stage. Science is 120 hours a year (3 hours a week), 240 hours a stage, so each focus area is about 30 hours.

| Stage 4 focus area | Hours | Stage 5 focus area | Hours |
|---|---|---|---|
| Observing the Universe | 30 | Energy | 30 |
| Forces | 30 | Disease | 30 |
| Cells and classification | 30 | Materials | 30 |
| Solutions and mixtures | 30 | Environmental sustainability | 30 |
| Living systems | 30 | Genetics and evolutionary change | 30 |
| Periodic table and atomic structure | 30 | Reactions | 30 |
| Change | 30 | Waves and motion | 30 |
| Data science 1 | 30 | Data science 2 | 30 |

Suggested order: one focus area per half term, two per term. Data science runs best alongside the other focus areas as practical investigations, not as a separate block.

### Outcome codes (Working scientifically, both stages)

SC4-WS-01 to SC4-WS-08 and SC5-WS-01 to SC5-WS-08: Observing, Questioning and predicting, Planning investigations, Conducting investigations, Processing data and information, Analysing data and information, Problem-solving, Communicating. Stage 5 versions add ethical, reliable and evidence-based requirements.

### Content outcome codes (captured so far)

Stage 4: SC4-OTU-01 (Observing the Universe), SC4-FOR-01 (Forces), SC4-CLS-01 (Cells and classification), SC4-SOL-01 (Solutions and mixtures), SC4-LIV-01 (Living systems), SC4-PRT-01 (Periodic table and atomic structure), SC4-CHG-01 (Change). Stage 5: SC5-EGY-01 (Energy). The remaining Stage 4 (Data science 1) and Stage 5 content codes are still to capture.

## Geography 7-10 (2024 syllabus, implemented from 2027)

Implemented in Years 7 and 9 in 2027, and Years 8 and 10 in 2028. Requires 10 hours of mandatory fieldwork. Geography is 60 hours a year, 120 a stage, so each focus area is about 30 hours, with fieldwork inside those hours.

| Stage 4 focus area | Hours | Stage 5 focus area | Hours |
|---|---|---|---|
| Landscapes and landforms | 30 | Biomes and sustainable agriculture | 30 |
| Liveability of places | 30 | Changing places | 30 |
| Water in the world | 30 | Environmental change and management | 30 |
| Interconnections and trade | 30 | Human wellbeing | 30 |

Each unit weaves in the geographical concepts (place, space, environment, interconnection, scale, sustainability, change), the inquiry skills (acquiring, processing and communicating geographical information) and the tools (maps, fieldwork, data and graphs, spatial technologies). Plan fieldwork as at least one local fieldwork task a year, totalling 10 hours across the stage.

## HSIE K-6 (2024 syllabus, implemented from 2027)

All focus areas must be taught across a stage. Time is 60 hours a year, 15 hours a term.

| Geography focus areas | History focus areas |
|---|---|
| People are connected to places | People have lived in the past |
| People are connected to places and groups | People learn about the past by engaging with stories, images, objects and sites |
| Geographical information is used to understand the world | History uses sources to construct narratives of the past |
| Geographical information is used to plan for sustainable futures | Historical sources present perspectives on the past |

Alternate Geography and History by term: four terms of each over a two-year stage. The Stage 2 sample unit themes are in stage-2.md. HSIE K-6 outcome codes are still to capture.
