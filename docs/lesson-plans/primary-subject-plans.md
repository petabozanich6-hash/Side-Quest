# Primary subject plans (Early Stage 1 to Stage 3)

Shared across all four primary stages. Hours per term are 40 teaching weeks in 4 terms of 10 weeks a year; Stages 1 to 3 repeat the pattern for a second year. All focus areas must be taught across a stage. Weekly splits are planning choices, not NESA requirements.

## English: Early Stage 1 and Stage 1

NESA English K-10 (2022) focus areas across the stages: Oral language and communication, Vocabulary, Phonological awareness, Print conventions, Phonic knowledge, Reading fluency, Reading comprehension, Creating written texts, Spelling, Handwriting, and Understanding and responding to literature. Early Stage 1 centres on phonological awareness, phonic knowledge and oral language; students who have not learnt the Early Stage 1 and Stage 1 phonics need explicit phonics instruction in later stages with decodable texts.

| Focus area | Hrs/week | Per term | Per year |
|---|---|---|---|
| Oral language and communication | 0.5 | 5 | 20 |
| Vocabulary | 0.5 | 5 | 20 |
| Phonological awareness | 0.25 | 2.5 | 10 |
| Print conventions | 0.25 | 2.5 | 10 |
| Phonic knowledge | 1 | 10 | 40 |
| Reading fluency | 0.25 | 2.5 | 10 |
| Reading comprehension | 0.5 | 5 | 20 |
| Creating written texts | 0.5 | 5 | 20 |
| Spelling | 0.25 | 2.5 | 10 |
| Handwriting | 0.5 | 5 | 20 |
| Understanding and responding to literature | 0.5 | 5 | 20 |
| **Total** | **5** | **50** | **200** |

Early Stage 1 suggested term weights: Term 1 phonological awareness, first single-letter sounds and oral language routines; Term 2 blending and segmenting simple words, digraphs; Term 3 more spelling patterns and narrative retell; Term 4 consolidation and simple decodable reading. In Stage 1, shift hours from phonological awareness and print conventions to reading comprehension and writing as the class is ready. Check the Stage 1 focus area list against the NESA outcomes page at build time.

## English: Stage 2 and Stage 3

See stage-2-english.md. Stage 3 uses the same focus areas and weekly split with more complex texts.

## Mathematics

Same 3 hours a week across all primary stages (30 hours a term). Focus areas by stage are in each stage file. Spread the 9 to 10 focus areas across the 8 terms, with Number and algebra taking about half of the hours.

## Science and Technology and HSIE

1.5 hours a week each, 15 hours a term, one sample unit per term over 8 terms (Early Stage 1: 4 terms). These are the 2024 syllabuses, implemented from 2027.

## PDHPE (K-6 syllabus 2024, from 2027)

Four focus areas: Movement skill and physical activity; Respectful relationships and safety; Identity, health and wellbeing; Self-management and interpersonal skills.

| Focus area | Per term (15 hrs) | Per year (60 hrs) | Whole stage (120 hrs) |
|---|---|---|---|
| Movement skill and physical activity | 4 | 16 | 32 |
| Respectful relationships and safety | 3 | 12 | 24 |
| Identity, health and wellbeing | 4 | 16 | 32 |
| Self-management and interpersonal skills | 4 | 16 | 32 |

Movement time can be daily short sessions rather than one block.

## Creative Arts (K-6 syllabus 2024, from 2027)

Four focus areas, each taught over a stage: Dance (Composing, Performing, Appreciating); Drama (Making, Performing, Appreciating); Music (Performing, Listening, Composing); Visual Arts (Making, Appreciating, Exhibiting).

| Term (any year) | Focus area | Hours |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Visual Arts | 15 |
| 2 | Music | 15 |
| 3 | Drama | 15 |
| 4 | Dance | 15 |

Each focus area gets 15 hours a year and 30 across a two-year stage. For Early Stage 1 (one year) each gets 15 hours. Swap the term order to suit the family.
