# Technology 7-8 and Visual Arts 7-10 outcome codes

Copied from the NESA NSW Curriculum outcomes pages. Paraphrased briefly.

## Technology 7-8 (2023, in use from 2026)

Technology Mandatory 7-8 (2017) is not available after December 2027. There are 8 Stage 4 outcomes and no Stage 5 outcomes in this syllabus. Stage 5 technology study uses the TAS elective syllabuses.

| Code | Outcome |
|---|---|
| TE4-SDP-01 | Explains relationships between sustainability, design and production |
| TE4-PDP-01 | Describes the practices and processes of designers and producers |
| TE4-MSC-01 | Explains how materials, systems and components contribute to solutions |
| TE4-PPM-01 | Applies processes in the planning, management and production of projects |
| TE4-DES-01 | Communicates and evaluates design ideas and solutions |
| TE4-SAF-01 | Selects and safely uses tools, materials, technologies and processes |
| TE4-DIG-01 | Demonstrates technological literacy to interact safely in digital environments |
| TE4-DIG-02 | Uses data and digital systems to code, design and produce projects |

Life Skills outcomes are TELS-SDP-01 and 02, PDP-01, MSC-01, PPM-01 to 03, DES-01, SAF-01, DIG-01 and 02.

## Visual Arts 7-10 (2024, from 2027)

The 2003 syllabus is not available after December 2028. Each stage has 6 outcomes: 3 for Art making and 3 for Art critical and historical studies, tied to Artworld concepts (C), Viewpoints (V) and Practice (P).

| Strand | Stage 4 | Stage 5 |
|---|---|---|
| Art making, concepts | VA4-AMC-01 | VA5-AMC-01 |
| Art making, viewpoints | VA4-AMV-01 | VA5-AMV-01 |
| Art making, practice | VA4-AMP-01 | VA5-AMP-01 |
| Critical and historical, concepts | VA4-CHC-01 | VA5-CHC-01 |
| Critical and historical, viewpoints | VA4-CHV-01 | VA5-CHV-01 |
| Critical and historical, practice | VA4-CHP-01 | VA5-CHP-01 |

Life Skills Stage 4/5 codes: VALS-AMC-01, AMV-01, AMP-01 to 03, CHC-01, CHV-01, CHP-01.

Plan Visual Arts term by term with one concept, one viewpoint and one practice outcome in each unit so that all six outcomes are covered each year.

## Drama 7-10 (2023, from 2026)

The 2003 syllabus is replaced. There are 3 Stage 4 outcomes and 6 Stage 5 outcomes, in three groups: making (dramatic contexts, processes and elements), performing and appreciating. The outcome codes did not appear in the page text, so they are still to capture.
