# Stage 2 term map (Years 3-4, 8 terms)

Master plan for every Stage 2 subject. Hours per term: English 50, Mathematics 30, Science and Technology 15, HSIE 15, PDHPE 15, Creative Arts 15, additional activities 30. Term order is a planning choice; subjects all need their focus areas taught across the two years.

## Term by term

| Term | Year | Mathematics focus (30 hrs) | Science and Technology (15 hrs) | HSIE (15 hrs) | PDHPE (15 hrs) | Creative Arts (15 hrs) |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | 3 | Representing numbers, Additive relations | Unit 1 | Climate zones | Movement skill and physical activity | Visual Arts |
| 2 | 3 | Geometric measure, Multiplicative relations | Unit 2 | Mayan, Aztec and Inca civilisations | Identity, health and wellbeing | Music |
| 3 | 3 | Partitioned fractions, 2D spatial structure | Unit 3 | Connections to Country | Respectful relationships and safety | Drama |
| 4 | 3 | 3D spatial structure, Non-spatial measure, Data | Unit 4 | Age of navigation and Terra Australis | Self-management and interpersonal skills | Dance |
| 5 | 4 | Representing numbers, Additive relations (Part B) | Unit 5 | Places organised, conservation and sustainability | Movement skill and physical activity | Visual Arts |
| 6 | 4 | Geometric measure, Multiplicative relations (Part B) | Unit 6 | First Fleet and the Gadigal Peoples | Respectful relationships and safety (includes online safety) | Music |
| 7 | 4 | Fractions, 2D spatial structure (Part B) | Unit 7 | Life at the Sydney Cove penal settlement | Identity, health and wellbeing | Drama |
| 8 | 4 | 3D spatial structure, Data, Chance | Unit 8 | Community needs, active citizenship, world religions | Self-management and interpersonal skills | Dance |

Science and Technology unit titles are still to copy from the NSW Department of Education Stage 2 sample units. English by term is in stage-2-english.md.
