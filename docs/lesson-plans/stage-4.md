# Stage 4 (Years 7-8)

40 teaching weeks a year, four terms of 10 weeks. A 2 unit style syllabus is about 120 hours a year, roughly 3 hours a week.

| Subject | Planned hrs/week | Planned hrs/year | Per term | One-hour lessons per year | Status |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| English | 4 | 160 | 40 | 160 | Not started |
| Mathematics | 4 | 160 | 40 | 160 | Not started |
| Science | 3 | 120 | 30 | 120 | Not started |
| History | 1.5 | 60 | 15 | 60 | Not started |
| Geography | 1.5 | 60 | 15 | 60 | Not started |
| Technology (Mandatory) | 3 | 120 | 30 | 120 | Not started |
| Language (100 hours, one 12-month period) | 2.5 | 100 | 25 | 100 | Not started |
| Elective 1 (for example PDHPE) | 3 | 120 | 30 | 120 | Not started |
| Elective 2 (for example Creative Arts) | 3 | 120 | 30 | 120 | Not started |

Rules to plan around:
- Compulsory: English, Mathematics, Science and HSIE (History and Geography).
- Choose 2 electives from different key learning areas: Creative Arts, PDHPE, Languages, Technology.
- 100 hours of one language over one continuous 12-month period is mandatory between Years 7 and 10, preferably in Years 7-8.

All rows together come to about 28 hours a week, so Language and Technology (Mandatory) can run in one year only, with the other rows adjusted around them. The Technology (Mandatory) hours are a planning figure and need checking against NESA requirements. Previous stage: Stage 3. Next stage: Stage 5.
