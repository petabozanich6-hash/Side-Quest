# Stage 4 (Years 7-8)

Two school years. Hours below are per school year, 4 terms of 10 weeks, planned for Year 7 and again for Year 8. A 2 unit style syllabus is about 120 hours a year, roughly 3 hours a week.

| Subject | Hrs/week | Per term | Per year | Lessons (1 hr) per year |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| English | 4 | 40 | 160 | 160 |
| Mathematics | 4 | 40 | 160 | 160 |
| Science | 3 | 30 | 120 | 120 |
| History | 1.5 | 15 | 60 | 60 |
| Geography | 1.5 | 15 | 60 | 60 |
| Technology (Mandatory) | 3 | 30 | 120 | 120 |
| Language (100 hours, one 12-month period) | 2.5 | 25 | 100 | 100 |
| Elective 1 (for example PDHPE) | 3 | 30 | 120 | 120 |
| Elective 2 (for example Creative Arts) | 3 | 30 | 120 | 120 |

Rules: compulsory English, Mathematics, Science and HSIE (History and Geography); two electives from different key learning areas (Creative Arts, PDHPE, Languages, Technology); 100 hours of one language over one continuous 12-month period is mandatory between Years 7 and 10, preferably Years 7-8.

All rows together come to about 28 hours a week, so the Language and Technology (Mandatory) rows run in one year only, with the others adjusted. The Technology (Mandatory) hours are a planning figure to confirm with NESA.

Status: time plan settled; subject focus areas open. Previous stage: Stage 3. Next stage: Stage 5.
