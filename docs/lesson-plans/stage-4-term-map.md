# Stage 4 term map (Years 7-8, 8 terms)

Hours per term: English 40, Mathematics 40, Science 30, History 15, Geography 15, Technology 7-8 30, PDHPE 30, Creative Arts elective 30, Language 25 (one 12-month period only, 4 terms). The language and the Creative Arts elective are the family's choice. Hour splits and term order are planning choices.

## Term by term

| Term | Year | Science (30 hrs) | History (15 hrs) | Geography (15 hrs) | Technology 7-8 (30 hrs) |

History case study (about 5 hours) and site study (about 5 hours) sit inside the context studies. Geography fieldwork (10 hours) sits inside the four focus areas. Note that History and Geography 7-10 (2024) start in 2027 and the Science 7-10 (2023) syllabus applies now.

## English and Mathematics (40 hrs per term each)

English: each term, split about 15 hours Reading, viewing and listening to texts, 10 hours Understanding and responding to texts, 15 hours Expressing ideas and composing texts. Choose a text focus per term.

Mathematics (planning choice, to check against the Stage 4/5 outcome groups): Year 7 T1 Number and finance, T2 Algebra and equations, T3 Length, area and volume, T4 Data classification, visualisation and analysis, and Probability. Year 8 T5 Ratios and rates, T6 Linear and non-linear relationships, T7 Geometrical properties and figures, T8 Pythagoras and trigonometry, with revision.

## Language, PDHPE and electives

Language: 25 hours a term for 4 consecutive terms (100 hours), usually in Year 7. PDHPE: 30 hours a term, alternating health and movement. Creative Arts elective: Visual Arts, Drama, Music or another course, 30 hours a term.
