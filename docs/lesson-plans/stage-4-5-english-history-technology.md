# English 7-10, History 7-10 and Technology 7-8 plans

Focus areas copied from the NESA NSW Curriculum site and NSW Department of Education pages. Hour splits are planning choices. Outcome codes for these three subjects are still to capture.

## English 7-10 (K-10 2022 syllabus)

Three focus areas in each of Stage 4 and Stage 5: Reading, viewing and listening to texts; Understanding and responding to texts; Expressing ideas and composing texts. All focus areas must be taught across a stage. English is 160 hours a year, 320 hours a stage.

| Focus area | Hrs/week | Hrs per year | Hrs per stage |
|---|---|---|---|
| Reading, viewing and listening to texts | 1.5 | 60 | 120 |
| Understanding and responding to texts | 1 | 40 | 80 |
| Expressing ideas and composing texts | 1.5 | 60 | 120 |
| **Total** | **4** | **160** | **320** |

Texts and literature requirements for Stages 4 and 5 are still to capture from the syllabus content page.

## History 7-10 (2024 syllabus, implemented from 2027)

Implemented in Years 7 and 9 in 2027 and Years 8 and 10 in 2028. The 2019 History K-10 syllabus is not available after December 2028. History is 60 hours a year, 120 hours a stage.

One case study and one site study are required in each stage, each about 5 hours, on a topic of choice aligned to one of the stage's focus areas. Stage 4 has a mandatory discrete outcome on Aboriginal Cultures and Histories. Stage 5 includes mandatory Australian civics and citizenship content.

| Stage 4 focus area | Hours | Stage 5 focus area | Hours |
|---|---|---|---|
| The ancient past (context study, plus depth study options) | 40 | The making of the modern world (core depth studies: Australia, making a nation from Federation to WWI; Australia at war, WWII) | 70 |
| The medieval world (context study, plus depth study options) | 40 | The modern world (core depth study: Human rights and freedoms) | 50 |
| The era of colonisation (core depth study: Aboriginal Peoples' experiences of colonisation) | 40 | | |
| **Stage total** | **120** | **Stage total** | **120** |

Stage 4: choose at least 2 depth studies from 16 options. Stage 5: choose at least 1 from 10 options. The case study and site study hours (10 a stage) sit inside the focus area hours above. Every focus area includes a content group called Working with historical concepts and skills, which includes writing.

## Technology 7-8 (2023 syllabus, implemented from 2026)

This replaces Technology Mandatory 7-8 (2017), which is not available after December 2027. Update the Stage 4 time plan label from Technology (Mandatory) to Technology 7-8. Technology is mandatory for all students in Years 7-8. Plan 120 hours a year, 240 a stage.

| Focus area | Hours per stage |
|---|---|
| Digital and communication technologies | 60 |
| Engineering technologies and systems | 60 |
| Food and agricultural practices | 60 |
| Materials and production processes | 60 |
| **Total** | **240** |

The course is built around practical experiences and design and production processes, so each focus area should include a designed and made project. The old 4 to 8 design projects and at least 6 different technologies rule applied to the 2017 syllabus and is not confirmed for the 2023 version.
