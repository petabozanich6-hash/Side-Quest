## Subject plans

- English: see stage-2-english.md.
- Mathematics focus areas: Number and algebra (Representing numbers using place value, Additive relations, Multiplicative relations, Partitioned fractions); Measurement and space (Geometric measure, 2D spatial structure, 3D spatial structure, Non-spatial measure); Statistics and probability (Data, Chance). All must be taught across the stage; some concepts may sit earlier or later in the two years.
- Science and Technology (2024 syllabus, from 2027): eight sample units, one a term. Outcome codes: ST2-SCI-01 (solar system and energy effects), ST2-PQU-01 (fair tests), ST2-DAT-01 (data patterns), ST2-DDT-01 (design process), ST2-DDT-02 (algorithms, data and digital systems). One sample unit covers landforms shaped by wind, water and living things, including Aboriginal and Torres Strait Islander stories about landscapes.
- HSIE (2024 syllabus, from 2027), eight sample units, one a term: climate zones; Mayan, Aztec and Inca civilisations; connections to Country; the age of navigation and Terra Australis; how places are organised and conservation; the First Fleet and the Gadigal Peoples; life in the Sydney Cove penal settlement; community needs, active citizenship and world religions in Australia.
- PDHPE and Creative Arts: focus areas not yet sourced.
