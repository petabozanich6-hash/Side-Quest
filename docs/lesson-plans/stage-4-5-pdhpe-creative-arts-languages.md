# PDHPE 7-10, Creative Arts 7-10 and Languages 7-10 plans

Copied from NESA NSW Curriculum and NSW Department of Education pages. Hour splits are planning choices. Outcome codes are still to capture for all of these.

## Languages 7-10

- Stage 4: 100 hours of one language over one continuous 12-month period is mandatory between Years 7 and 10, preferably in Years 7-8.
- Stage 5: a language can be elected, with a 200 hour course.
- Four language syllabuses exist: Aboriginal Languages K-10, Auslan K-10, Classical Languages K-10 and Modern Languages K-10 (2022). Which language is offered depends on available resources, so the family chooses the language.
- Modern Languages: 'Interacting' is mainly oral, exchanging and negotiating meaning, so plan speaking practice every week.
- NSW Department sample scope and sequences exist for Stage 4 (100 hours) and Stage 5 (200 hours elective).

| Course | Hrs/week | Hours | Per term |
|---|---|---|---|
| Stage 4 mandatory | 2.5 | 100 (one 12-month period) | 25 |
| Stage 5 elective | 5 | 200 | 50 |

The Stage 5 elective at 5 hours a week is a heavy load; spread across both Stage 5 years it is about 2.5 hours a week.

## Creative Arts 7-10

- Visual Arts 7-10 (2024) is implemented from 2027, and the 2003 syllabus is not available after December 2028. Six focus areas: Art making (Artworld concepts, Viewpoints, Practice) and Art critical and historical studies (Artworld concepts, Viewpoints, Practice). They are taught together. Drawing is mandatory in Stage 4. Stage 5 students build a body of work across the stage. Use a Visual Arts diary.
- Drama 7-10 (2023) is implemented from 2026. Three focus areas: Making, Performing, Appreciating.
- Other Creative Arts syllabuses: Dance, Music, Photographic and Digital Media, Visual Design.

| Plan | Hrs/week | Hours per year | Hours per stage |
|---|---|---|---|
| One Creative Arts elective | 3 | 120 | 240 |

Visual Arts split: Art making about 60 percent of hours (about 72 a year) and Art critical and historical studies about 40 percent (about 48 a year). Drama split: Making, Performing and Appreciating equal thirds (40 hours each a year). Mandatory Creative Arts hours in Stage 4, if any, still need checking against NESA requirements.

## PDHPE 7-10

- The PDHPE K-10 (2018) syllabus can be taught through 2026, with a staggered move to a new syllabus from 2027. The new syllabus's focus areas are not captured here.
- The NSW Department publishes Stage 4 and Stage 5 sample scope and sequences, covering health choices, nutrition, mental health, body image, respectful relationships, consent and digital health literacy.
- Related elective courses: Child Studies 7-10 (2025) and Physical Activity and Sports Studies 7-10 (2019), both Content Endorsed Courses.

| Plan | Hrs/week | Hours per year | Hours per stage |
|---|---|---|---|
| PDHPE | 3 | 120 | 240 |

Split roughly into health and wellbeing theory (about 50 percent) and movement and physical activity (about 50 percent) until the new syllabus's focus areas are captured.
