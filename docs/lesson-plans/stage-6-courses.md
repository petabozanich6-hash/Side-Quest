# Stage 6 course plans

Copied from NESA and NSW Department of Education pages. Each 2 unit course is 120 hours in Year 11 and 120 hours in Year 12, about 3 hours a week and 30 hours a term. Hour splits within a course are planning choices. Outcome codes are still to capture.

## English Advanced 11-12 (2024)

Students study a range of focus areas over 120 indicative hours in Year 11 and 120 in Year 12. The HSC examination has 2 written papers across 2 days. The focus area names and module list are still to capture from the syllabus content page.

## Mathematics Advanced 11-12 (2024, implemented from 2026)

Seven areas of study over 240 hours of course time: Functions, Trigonometric functions, Exponential and logarithmic functions, Sequences and series, Calculus, Statistical analysis, Financial mathematics. The HSC exam is based on the Year 12 outcomes, with Year 11 assumed knowledge that may be examined.

NSW Department sample units give lesson counts to use as a guide. Year 11: probability and data 8 lessons, introduction to functions 7, graphing transformations 12, foundations of differentiation 8, trigonometry 19. Year 12: further differentiation, modelling and graphing 11, differentiation applications 9.

## Mathematics Standard 11-12 (2024)

Five areas of study over 120 hours in Year 11 and 120 hours in Year 12. Year 11 is a common course for students going into Year 12 Mathematics Standard 1 or Mathematics Standard 2. The HSC exam is based on Mathematics Standard 2 (one 2.5 hour written paper, 100 marks), with Year 11 assumed knowledge. Assessment weights Knowledge and understanding and Skills in working mathematically at 50 percent each. The five area names are still to capture.
