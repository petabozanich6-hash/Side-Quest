# Stage 2 English (Years 3-4)

Built on the NESA English K-10 Syllabus (2022), Years 3-6 focus areas. Time: 200 hours a year, 5 hours a week, 50 hours a term (see stage-2.md).

Status: outline only. Syllabus outcome codes and content points for Stage 2 are not yet copied in; add them from the NESA Stage 2 content page before writing lessons.

## Focus areas and hours

The syllabus says all focus areas must be taught across a stage. The split below is a planning choice, not a NESA requirement.

| Focus area | Hrs/week | Hrs/term | Hrs/year |
|---|---|---|---|
| Oral language and communication | 0.5 | 5 | 20 |
| Vocabulary | 0.5 | 5 | 20 |
| Reading fluency | 0.5 | 5 | 20 |
| Reading comprehension | 1 | 10 | 40 |
| Creating written texts | 1 | 10 | 40 |
| Spelling | 0.5 | 5 | 20 |
| Handwriting and digital transcription | 0.5 | 5 | 20 |
| Understanding and responding to literature | 0.5 | 5 | 20 |
| **Total** | **5** | **50** | **200** |

## Daily requirements

NESA requires daily opportunities across the year to be read to, for wide and deep reading, and for wide writing. Build these into every week, not as separate units.

## Text requirements across the year

The year's texts must include texts by Aboriginal and Torres Strait Islander Peoples, Australian literature, and literature from other countries. Literature must include novels, plays, poetry, myths, legends, fables and fairytales, information texts, persuasive texts, hybrid texts, and texts chosen by the student.

## Term plan (suggested)

| Term | Writing and text focus | Literature to include | Lessons |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Imaginative texts: narrative, character, setting | A picture book, a myth or fable, a text by an Aboriginal or Torres Strait Islander author | 50 |
| 2 | Informative texts: reports, explanations, using sources | An information text, a novel read aloud over the term | 50 |
| 3 | Persuasive texts: opinion, argument, evidence | Poetry and wordplay, an Australian text | 50 |
| 4 | Hybrid texts, plays, review and extension of the year | A play, a text from another country, a student-chosen text | 50 |

## Lesson plan

200 one-hour lessons, 5 a week for 40 weeks. Suggested week pattern, matching the hours above:

- Lesson 1: Reading comprehension
- Lesson 2: Creating written texts
- Lesson 3: Creating written texts (editing and publishing) or Reading comprehension
- Lesson 4: Literature, with vocabulary and oral language
- Lesson 5: Spelling, handwriting and digital transcription, with reading fluency

Lesson codes: S2-ENG-T[term]-W[week]-L[lesson], for example S2-ENG-T1-W1-L1.
