# Stage 2 English (Years 3-4)

NESA English K-10 Syllabus (2022), Years 3-6 focus areas. Stage 2 is two years, 8 terms. English time: 5 hours a week, 50 hours a term, 200 hours a year, 400 hours for the stage.

## Focus areas and hours

All focus areas must be taught across the stage. The split is a planning choice, not a NESA requirement.

| Focus area | Hrs/week | Per term | Per year | Whole stage |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Oral language and communication | 0.5 | 5 | 20 | 40 |
| Vocabulary | 0.5 | 5 | 20 | 40 |
| Reading fluency | 0.5 | 5 | 20 | 40 |
| Reading comprehension | 1 | 10 | 40 | 80 |
| Creating written texts | 1 | 10 | 40 | 80 |
| Spelling | 0.5 | 5 | 20 | 40 |
| Handwriting and digital transcription | 0.5 | 5 | 20 | 40 |
| Understanding and responding to literature | 0.5 | 5 | 20 | 40 |
| **Total** | **5** | **50** | **200** | **400** |

## Daily requirements

Daily opportunities to be read to, for wide and deep reading and for wide writing, built into every week.

## Text requirements across the stage

Texts by Aboriginal and Torres Strait Islander Peoples, Australian literature and literature from other countries; novels, plays, poetry, myths, legends, fables and fairytales, information texts, persuasive texts, hybrid texts and student-chosen texts.

## Term plan (suggested)

| Term | Year | Writing and text focus | Literature to include |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | 3 | Imaginative texts: narrative, character, setting | Picture book, a myth or fable, Aboriginal or Torres Strait Islander author |
| 2 | 3 | Informative texts: reports, explanations | Information text, a novel read aloud |
| 3 | 3 | Persuasive texts: opinion, evidence | Poetry, an Australian text |
| 4 | 3 | Hybrid texts and review of the year | A play, a student-chosen text |
| 5 | 4 | Imaginative texts with more complex structure | A legend or fairytale, a text from another country |
| 6 | 4 | Informative texts using several sources | Information text, a novel studied over the term |
| 7 | 4 | Persuasive texts and responding to arguments | Poetry, an Australian text |
| 8 | 4 | Hybrid texts, extension and review of the stage | A play, a student-chosen text |

Lessons: 400 one-hour lessons, 5 a week. Week pattern: reading comprehension; creating written texts; creating written texts or reading comprehension; literature with vocabulary and oral language; spelling, handwriting and digital transcription with reading fluency. Lesson codes: S2-ENG-T[term 1-8]-W[week]-L[lesson].
