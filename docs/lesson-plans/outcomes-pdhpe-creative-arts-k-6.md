# PDHPE and Creative Arts K-6 outcome codes (2024 syllabuses)

Copied from the NESA NSW Curriculum site with short paraphrased descriptions. Both syllabuses are implemented from 2027. The outcomes pages list Early Stage 1, Stage 1 and Stage 2 only; Stage 3 is not shown, so confirm what applies to Years 5-6 with NESA. The previous PDHPE K-10 (2018) and Creative Arts K-6 (2006) syllabuses are not available after December 2028.

## PDHPE K-6 (2024)

| Focus area | Early Stage 1 | Stage 1 | Stage 2 |
|---|---|---|---|
| Movement skill and physical activity | PHE-MSP-01 (fundamental movement skills, joining in activities) | PH1-MSP-01 (movement skills and fair play) | PH2-MSP-01 (movement skills, strategies, teamwork) |
| Respectful relationships and safety | PHE-RRS-01 (how relationships and safety support wellbeing) | PH1-RRS-01 (respectful and safe actions offline and online) | PH2-RRS-01 (strengthening respectful relationships), PH2-RRS-02 (interacting safely offline and online) |
| Identity, health and wellbeing | PHE-IHW-01 (factors affecting identity, health, wellbeing) | PH1-IHW-01 (describes those factors) | PH2-IHW-01 (explains how factors influence each other) |
| Self-management and interpersonal skills | PHE-SMI-01 | PH1-SMI-01 | PH2-SMI-01 |

Stage 2 has one extra outcome (a second Respectful relationships and safety outcome), so give that focus area a little more time in Stage 2 than in earlier stages.

## Creative Arts K-6 (2024)

| Focus area | Early Stage 1 | Stage 1 | Stage 2 |
|---|---|---|---|
| Dance | CAE-DAN-01 | CA1-DAN-01 | CA2-DAN-01 |
| Drama | CAE-DRA-01 | CA1-DRA-01 | CA2-DRA-01 |
| Music | CAE-MUS-01 | CA1-MUS-01 | CA2-MUS-01 |
| Visual Arts | CAE-VIS-01 | CA1-VIS-01 | CA2-VIS-01 |

Early Stage 1 outcomes are about experimenting with and identifying; Stage 1 adds making, performing and composing; Stage 2 adds an audience. There is one outcome per art form, so planning each focus area as a 15 hour term block a year fits one outcome with its three practices.
