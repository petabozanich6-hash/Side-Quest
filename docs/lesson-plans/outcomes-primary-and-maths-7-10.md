# Outcome code maps

Outcome codes copied from the NESA NSW Curriculum site, with short paraphrased descriptions. Use these codes in the lesson template's outcome field. Stage 3 outcome codes were not captured and are still to add. Check wording against the NESA site before relying on it.

In Stages 2 and 3, content is split into Part A (early concepts) and Part B (builds on A). Stage 2 Part A does not equal Year 3 only. Teachers choose from A and B by prior learning, so Year 3 and Year 4 plans should both start from a check of what the student knows.

## Mathematics 7-10 structure

Stage 4 and 5 Core: Number and finance, Algebra and equations, Ratios and rates, Linear and non-linear relationships, Pythagoras and trigonometry, Length, area and volume, Geometrical properties and figures, Data classification, visualisation and analysis, Probability.

Stage 5 Paths: Further algebra and equations, Variation and rates of change, Functions and graphs, Further trigonometry, Further area and volume, Geometrical figures and proof, Introduction to networks, Data analysis and statistical enquiry, Further probability.

The Core typically covers learning up to the middle of Stage 5, and Paths can be programmed at any time in Stages 4 and 5. The Core gives the foundation for Mathematics Standard in Years 11-12. Plan Stage 4 as Core, and Stage 5 as the rest of Core plus the Paths chosen to suit the student.
