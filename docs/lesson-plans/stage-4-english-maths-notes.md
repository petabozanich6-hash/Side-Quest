# Stage 4 English and Mathematics outcome codes

The NESA outcomes pages for English K-10 and Mathematics K-10 (2022) show only up to Stage 2 in the page text. These Stage 4 lists come from teaching sites that agree with each other, and should be checked against the NESA Stage 4 tabs before lessons are written.

## English 7-10 (6 outcomes in Stage 4, 6 in Stage 5)

Pattern: EN + stage + focus area + number. Stage 5 uses the same letters with EN5.

| Focus area | Stage 4 code | What it covers |
|---|---|---|
| Reading, viewing and listening to texts | EN4-RVL-01 | Personal, creative and critical reading strategies for texts complex in ideas and construction |
| Understanding and responding to texts A | EN4-URA-01 | How meaning is made through language forms, features and structures |
| Understanding and responding to texts B | EN4-URB-01 | Still to confirm wording |
| Understanding and responding to texts C | EN4-URC-01 | Still to confirm wording |
| Expressing ideas and composing texts A | EN4-ECA-01 | Creating personal, creative and critical texts using the conventions of language |
| Expressing ideas and composing texts B | EN4-ECB-01 | Planning, monitoring, revising and reflecting when composing |

## Mathematics 7-10 (16 outcomes in Stage 4, all Core)

Every Stage 4 code has C in the middle, so there is no Path outcome in Stage 4.

| Strand | Codes |
|---|---|
| Number and algebra | MA4-INT-C-01 integers, MA4-FRC-C-01 fractions, decimals and percentages, MA4-IND-C-01 indices, MA4-RAT-C-01 ratios and rates, MA4-ALG-C-01 algebraic techniques (two-step limit), MA4-LIN-C-01 linear relationships, MA4-EQU-C-01 equations |
| Measurement and space | MA4-LEN-C-01 length, MA4-ARE-C-01 area, MA4-VOL-C-01 volume, MA4-ANG-C-01 angle relationships, MA4-GEO-C-01 properties of geometrical figures, MA4-PYT-C-01 Pythagoras' theorem |
| Statistics and probability | MA4-DAT-C-01 data classification and visualisation, MA4-DAT-C-02 data analysis, MA4-PRO-C-01 probability |

## Mathematics term order (replaces the earlier outline in stage-4-term-map.md)

Two outcomes a term, about 20 hours each, 40 hours a term. Order is a planning choice that follows the usual teaching sequence.

| Term | Year | Outcomes |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | 7 | MA4-INT-C-01, MA4-FRC-C-01 |
| 2 | 7 | MA4-IND-C-01, MA4-RAT-C-01 |
| 3 | 7 | MA4-ALG-C-01, MA4-EQU-C-01 |
| 4 | 7 | MA4-LIN-C-01, MA4-ANG-C-01 |
| 5 | 8 | MA4-GEO-C-01, MA4-DAT-C-01 |
| 6 | 8 | MA4-DAT-C-02, MA4-PRO-C-01 |
| 7 | 8 | MA4-LEN-C-01, MA4-ARE-C-01 |
| 8 | 8 | MA4-VOL-C-01, MA4-PYT-C-01 |

Build in revision of fractions, ratios and algebra in Year 8, because they are revisited in later outcomes.
