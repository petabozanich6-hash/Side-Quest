# Stage 4 English and Mathematics outcome notes

The NESA outcomes pages for English K-10 (2022) and Mathematics K-10 (2022) show only Early Stage 1 to Stage 2 in the page text, so Stage 4 and Stage 5 codes have not been verified from NESA.

## English 7-10

Source to use: the NSW Department of Education English 7-10 outcomes and content tracker (XLSX, aligned to the NESA 2022 syllabus), which covers Stages 4 and 5 and Life Skills.

Codes seen on a third-party site, not yet verified against NESA: EN4-ECA-01, EN4-ECB-01, EN4-RVL-01, EN4-URA-01, EN4-URB-01, EN4-URC-01. Stage 4 has 9 outcomes in total, so three codes are still unseen.

## Mathematics 7-10

Stage 4/5 core outcome groups (from the NESA course overview): Number and finance, Algebra and equations, Ratios and rates, Linear and non-linear relationships, Pythagoras and trigonometry, Length, area and volume, Geometrical properties and figures, Data classification, visualisation and analysis, and Probability.

Codes seen on a third-party site, not yet verified against NESA: MA4-ANG-C-01, MA4-ARE-C-01, MA4-DAT-C-01, MA4-EQU-C-01. The C in the code marks a core outcome, which gives the foundation for Mathematics Standard 2 in Stage 6.
