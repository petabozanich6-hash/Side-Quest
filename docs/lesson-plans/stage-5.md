# Stage 5 (Years 9-10)

40 teaching weeks a year, four terms of 10 weeks. A 2 unit style syllabus is about 120 hours a year, roughly 3 hours a week.

| Subject | Planned hrs/week | Planned hrs/year | Per term | One-hour lessons per year | Status |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| English | 4 | 160 | 40 | 160 | Not started |
| Mathematics | 4 | 160 | 40 | 160 | Not started |
| Science (one course across Years 7-10) | 3 | 120 | 30 | 120 | Not started |
| History | 1.5 | 60 | 15 | 60 | Not started |
| Geography | 1.5 | 60 | 15 | 60 | Not started |
| Language (if elected or not yet completed) | 2.5 | 100 | 25 | 100 | Not started |
| Elective 1 | 3 | 120 | 30 | 120 | Not started |
| Elective 2 | 3 | 120 | 30 | 120 | Not started |

Stage 5 electives come from Creative Arts, PDHPE, Languages, Technology and Vocational Education and Training (VET), and must be from different key learning areas. Build lessons for each elective the family chooses, rather than all of them at once. Previous stage: Stage 4. Next stage: Stage 6.
