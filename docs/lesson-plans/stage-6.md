# Stage 6 (Years 11-12)

A unit is 60 hours a year, so a 2 unit course is 120 hours a year, about 3 hours a week over 40 weeks, 30 hours a term.

## Pattern of study

- Year 11 (Preliminary): at least 12 units including 2 units of English. Example: six 2 unit courses, 720 hours, about 18 hours a week.
- Year 12 (HSC): at least 10 units including 2 units of English. Example: five 2 unit courses, 600 hours, about 15 hours a week.
- Both years: a 2 unit English course, at least 6 units of NESA developed courses, 3 courses of 2 unit value, and at least 4 subjects.

## Time allocation

| Course type | Hrs/week | Per term | Per year | Lessons (1 hr) per year |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| English (compulsory, 2 units) | 3 | 30 | 120 | 120 |
| Each other 2 unit course | 3 | 30 | 120 | 120 |
| 1 unit extension course | 1.5 | 15 | 60 | 60 |

Each course is planned twice, once for Preliminary and once for HSC. Courses are listed on the Lessons page. Several syllabuses (English, Mathematics Standard, Science, History) are new, so check the current course description. Plan only the courses the family chooses.
