# Stage 6 (Years 11-12)

A unit is 60 hours a year, so a 2 unit course is 120 hours a year, about 3 hours a week over 40 weeks, or 30 hours a term.

## Pattern of study

- Year 11 (Preliminary): at least 12 units, including 2 units of English. Example: six 2 unit courses, 720 hours, about 18 hours a week.
- Year 12 (HSC): at least 10 units, including 2 units of English. Example: five 2 unit courses, 600 hours, about 15 hours a week.
- Both years: a 2 unit English course, at least 6 units of NESA developed courses, 3 courses of 2 unit value, and at least 4 subjects.

## Time allocation

| Course type | Hrs/week | Hrs/year | Per term | One-hour lessons per year | Status |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| English (compulsory, 2 units) | 3 | 120 | 30 | 120 | Not started |
| Each other 2 unit course | 3 | 120 | 30 | 120 | Not started |
| 1 unit extension course | 1.5 | 60 | 15 | 60 | Not started |

Each course is built twice, once for Preliminary (Year 11) and once for HSC (Year 12). Courses are listed on the Lessons page under Stage 6. Several syllabuses (English, Mathematics Standard, Science, History) are new, so check the current course description before planning. Build only the courses the family chooses.
